PlantVisionBD: Plant Disease Detection Using EfficientNetV2L with Explainable AI

Abdullah · Md. Kamran Hasan Rafin · Suvrom Sen · Khalid Bin Walid — Dept. of CSE, Southeast University, Dhaka

Co-built a plant disease classifier covering 38 crop-disease categories across 14 species, trained on roughly 87,800 leaf images. Refactored and hardened the training pipeline around an EfficientNetV2L backbone with a three-phase progressive fine-tuning strategy, GeM pooling, Mixup/CutMix augmentation, EMA weight averaging, and 10x test-time augmentation — reaching 98.43% test accuracy. Along the way, identified and fixed nine bugs in the original baseline notebook, the most critical being a double-normalization error that was silently zeroing every training gradient.

Layered Grad-CAM and SHAP on top of the model to confirm it attends to actual leaf lesions rather than background artifacts, and ran a failure analysis on misclassified samples tracing the residual errors to genuinely ambiguous, visually-similar disease pairs.
